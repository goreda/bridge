Kern/overlap schemas (actual headers from engine outputs and repo fixtures @ PR 84fbe2fa).

1) out/kern-ledger.tsv (per requested kern pair):
   left  right  requested  written  status  reason
   - requested/written in font units; status ready|held; reason carries the typed collision/solver detail
     (e.g. EXACT_ORIGIN_COLLISION_RESOLVED_ON_LATTICE with origin penetration and shipped lattice integer).

2) out/spacing-pair-ledger.tsv (per ordered pair, solver view):
   left  right  class  reason  tier  t_lo  t_hi  residual  j_star  kern_units  natural_safety
   - self-pairs are explicit: class=self, reason=excluded_self_pair (self-overlap is structurally
     excluded from the pair solve, never silently kerned).

3) out/spacing.tsv (per glyph):
   glyph_id  lsb  rsb  advance

4) engine/metrics/tests/fixtures/kern-verified-rows-schema.tsv (measurement/verification rows):
   font_sha256  left  right  outline_source  outline_sha256  upm  gap_after  gap_target
   y_lo  y_hi  error_bound_method  error_bound_value  tol  tolerance_label  status  reason
