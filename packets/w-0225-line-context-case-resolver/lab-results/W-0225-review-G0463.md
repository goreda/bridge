## Observations (not acceptance gates)
1. Identical calibration and validation aggregates mean the reported aggregates match. They do not show that validation added no information; the suite is narrow (one scenario produces all hints) and the thresholds are conservative. I did not see per-row comparison between the two stages beyond identical counts.
2. The descriptive agreement is mostly a property of the fixture: the one scenario that hints alternates small and capital pairs of known scale. It says nothing about real lines. Mixed all-62 lines never resolve (digits, descenders and bottom spread push to abstain), so real mixed text is not covered by the stored evidence.
3. Rotation/uneven handling is a proxy: abstain when ink-bottom spread exceeds .06 of the tallest ink height. My probes: 6 regions at 0.3, 0.6, 1.0 px baseline drift per glyph still resolved; 2.0 px abstained. The report says tiny tilt can pass. The stored rotated and uneven fixtures abstain at every row (240 and 240 in calibration/validation, 1392 each in test). So the packet's abstention rule is supported by those fixtures and my probes, not proven for arbitrary rotation.
4. Chronology rests on archive-internal times. tar stores second-level mtimes (listed with --full-time): case_resolver.py 19:22:33; line_harness.py 19:23:28; calibration outputs 19:23:31; freeze.json 19:23:42.986 (equals freeze_time in the file); validation outputs 19:23:45.6; test outputs 19:24:03.8; ACCESS-RECEIPT 19:24:48; REPORT.md and delivery manifest 19:32:46-47; our freeze public 19:27:19. By these file times the module and harness precede the freeze, the freeze precedes the validation and test outputs, and all heldout outputs precede our freeze. These are file-system times in an archive the solver built, not signed or independent proof, and mtimes can be set. Calibration had two attempts (freeze.json: harness-only change to add actual 5-degree rotation before freeze); I cannot verify no heldout access occurred earlier than these times. The solver statement that our list arrived after the heldout runs is consistent with these times and is not independently proven.
5. Pixel evidence covers 12 sampled lines only. The 1170 full line images were not provided.
6. Fixture caveat: the labels lower_like and upper_like name height groups only. The report states this; downstream use should keep the reason string.
7. Minor: the heldout contact sheet does not say which stage each line came from.
